Ask a Question
How does Belleza Team work?

Belleza Team helps individuals learn how to build an online-focused business through training, mentorship, community support and practical guidance. Our activities include opportunities connected with NeoLife and our online FHG activities.

Can I participate online?

Yes. A significant part of the Belleza Team business can be conducted online, including communication, training, networking and sharing information.

Do I need previous experience?

No previous business or marketing experience is required to ask questions and learn about the opportunity. New members can learn step by step through available training and support.

How do I get started?

Contact Belleza Team and tell us whether you are interested in NeoLife, the online FHG business or both. We can explain the process and the appropriate next steps.

What is the FHG business?

FHG is an online business opportunity that forms part of Belleza Team’s activities. We help interested individuals understand the online system, processes, training and support involved. Specific activities and requirements depend on the FHG program.

How does NeoLife fit into the business?

NeoLife is one of the businesses Belleza Team partners with. NeoLife provides wellness products and a direct-selling business opportunity. We help interested people learn about the products and understand the associated opportunity.
